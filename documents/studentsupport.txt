Miner Support
At UTEP, we're dedicated to supporting you throughout your academic journey. From emergency financial aid to tech equipment, housing assistance, class registration help, career resources, and beyond, we've got you covered. If you need further guidance other than the resources listed below, reach out to our CARE Team at minersupport@utep.edu or give us a call at (915) 747-5076. We are here to assist you with any questions or concerns you may have.
